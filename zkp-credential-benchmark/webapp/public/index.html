ZK Credential Demo NoirJS + bb.js (client-side proving) → ethers.js (on-chain verification)
This page generates the same four credential circuits benchmarked in Chapter 3/4 of the thesis, entirely in your browser (no server ever sees your private attributes), then submits the proof to a deployed verifier contract on either a local Anvil node or Sepolia. See webapp/README.md for setup steps.
